Meet the sounds

i and t

Ii
/ɪ/
short i — as in it

How to say it

Short and relaxed. Don’t smile, don’t stretch the lips. It is the i in German bitte, Mitte, ist.

Listen and repeat

German trap: “ie”Your German eyes want a long “ie” /iː/. In a short English word, i is always short: is not , is not . The long sound is usually spelled ee or ea.
Tt
/t/
t — as in top

How to say it

The tip of your tongue is behind your top teeth. Then a short puff of air: t! The same as German. Easy!

Listen and repeat

Don’t swallow the t!Say the t at the end clearly: it, sit, cat. th (this, think) is a different sound — a later lesson.

Where does i say /ɪ/?

Tap the words

Tap every word where i says short /ɪ/, like in it. Read them aloud first!

it · time · pin · pine · tip · ice · sit · site · fish · bike · tin · nice · film · kite

Did you notice?When a word ends in e (time, pine, site), the i says its name /aɪ/. This is the magic e trick. It comes in a later lesson.
