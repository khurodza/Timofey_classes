👩‍🏫 Teacher only · Exercise 1 (1.06)
Teacher’s Book: Check/clarify: fan. Tell students to cover the dialogue and ask “Which singer is on the girl’s T-shirt?” (Ariana Grande). Tell students not to focus on understanding every word for now, but to focus on listening for the answer. Answer to the book question: Camila.
  • The dialogue is folded away on purpose (= “cover the dialogue”). First listening: picture + audio only. Second listening: he opens “Now read and listen again”.
  • Ask Why Camila? → Steve thinks Shawn Mendes is British; Camila knows he’s from Canada.
  • Ask him to find three questions in the dialogue (→ Exercise 3) and the phrase Nice to meet you, too (→ Exercise 4).
Culture notes: Ariana Grande — American singer-songwriter and actress, born 1993; famous from the TV series Victorious (Cat Valentine); first album Yours Truly (2013) went to number one. Shawn Mendes — Canadian singer-songwriter, born 1998; started by posting song covers online in 2013; album Handwritten (2015) went to number one before he was 18.

👩‍🏫 Teacher only · Read it right: e (from Lesson 2)
Same tab as in Lesson 2, continuing the order s·a → c·n → o·p (Lesson 9) → e. Clips: Kokoro af_bella (American English), as in Lesson 2.
  • Steps 1–2: short /e/ vs /æ/ (pen / pan, bed / bad) — open the mouth wider for /æ/. Check the four-vowel listening (pit / pet / pat / pot).
  • Step 3 (boss level): magic silent e — tap → tape, not → note. Listen for a pronounced final “-eh” (time as “tee-meh”).
  • Steps 4–5: dictation and riddles; if time is short, he finishes them at home.
  • Link to today: tennis, best, Spain, name, Rome — ask which words have a magic e (name, Rome).

The magic silent e

In German you say every e: Name, Hose, Katze. In English, an e at the end is usually silent…
