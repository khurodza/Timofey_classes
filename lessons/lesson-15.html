Number quiz

Exercise 9 · Quiz time

The great big number quiz!!

4 questions. Guess first, then check. How many can you get?

1

The number of countries in the world is …

🌍 193 countries are in the United Nations, plus 2 more: the Vatican and Palestine.

2

… million people speak English as their first language.

🗣️ And over 1.5 billion people speak English as a second language. Like you!

3

There are … seconds in a year.

⏱️ 365 days × 24 hours × 60 minutes × 60 seconds = 31,536,000.

4

… million people have the same birthday as you.

🎂 About 8 billion people ÷ 365 days ≈ 20 million birthday twins!

🎧 Listen and check.

Say the big numbers
