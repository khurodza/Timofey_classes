A digraph is a team of two letters that make one sound.

Диграф — это команда из двух букв, которые дают один звук.

Don’t read the letters one by one: s + h is not “s-h”. Together they are one new sound. Today: sh → ch → ph → ck → th.

Meet the sound

ch

ch
/tʃ/
as in chips
c+h→ch=/tʃ/

How to say it

Start with a quick t and go straight into sh: t + sh → “tsh”. It is the tsch in German Deutsch, tschüss, Matsch.

Key words

More words: listen and repeat

German trap: not “ich”, not “ach”English ch is never the soft sound in ich or the hard sound in Buch. Say “tsch”: , , .
tch = the same soundAfter a short vowel you often see three letters: . The t is already inside the sound.
