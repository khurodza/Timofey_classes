Meet the sounds

s and a

Ss
/s/
the snake sound — as in sun

How to say it

Teeth close together, air hisses out: sssss. Touch your throat: no buzz! (The buzzing sound is /z/.)

Listen and repeat

German trap 1: s + vowelIn German, s before a vowel buzzes: Sonne, sehen. In English, s at the start of a word never buzzes: is not , is not .
German trap 2: st and spIn German, Stein, Sport start with “sht”, “shp”. In English, st and sp are a clean s + t, s + p: .
Small exception: in is, as, has, his the s buzzes like /z/ . Don’t say “iss”!
Aa
/æ/
short a — as in cat

How to say it

This sound is not in German. Open your mouth wide (two fingers fit between your teeth), keep your tongue low and forward. It’s between German a and ä. Short!

Listen and repeat

German trap: a / eDon’t say a German “a” or an “e”. Listen: or , or , or .
The name of the letter A is “ay”, but in short words it says /æ/.
