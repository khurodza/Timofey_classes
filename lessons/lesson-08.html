Pronunciation · Student’s Book p. 120 · 1.03–1.04

/h/ or /w/ in question words

Exercise 1 · 1.03

Read and listen to the questions.

How old are you?

Where are you from?

What’s your favourite food?

Who’s your favourite football player?

Why do you like him?

Two sounds, not three!Who and How start with /h/ — just a soft breath, like warming your hands. Not a hard Russian «х»!
What, Where, Why start with /w/ — round your lips like for «у». Not «в»: your teeth don’t touch your lip.
Careful: Who is /huː/ — the w is silent.
