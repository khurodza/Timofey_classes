Speaking

Let’s talk

Speaking

Your teacher asks. You answer. Say as much as you can!

  1. What’s your name? How old are you?
  2. Have you got any brothers, sisters or pets? Tell me about them.
  3. What can you do well? What can’t you do?
  4. What do you usually do after school?
  5. What did you do last weekend?
  6. What do you like more: films, books or games? Why?
  7. What are you going to do in the summer holidays?
  8. Have you ever been to another country? When did you go? What was it like?
  9. What will you do this weekend if the weather is bad?
  10. What would you do if you had a million dollars?
    – or – Tell me about something you’ve done that you’re proud of.
