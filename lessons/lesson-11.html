👩‍🏫 Teacher only · Exercise 9 + Look box
Teacher’s Book: Remind students that we use an article with a noun and that the adjective comes between the article and the noun. Elicit or explain that there is no article with shirts and tickets because they are plural.
Answers: 1 a new T-shirt · 2 an expensive restaurant · 3 a fast car · 4 clean shoes · 5 a cheap hotel
Language note (T17) · Indefinite articles: a / an go before a singular noun when the thing is not already known (Do you have a cat?) — compare the for something known (Where is the cat?). a /ə/ before a consonant sound (a car, a big apple), an /ən/ before a vowel sound (an old car, an apple). Spelling can trick: a university, a one-year-old child.
For a Russian speaker: no articles in Russian → expect *I have old bike; and the “Look” box matters less (Russian adjectives agree, English never: *olds shoes).
The free-writing line previews have got (Unit 2) — accept I have … too.

👩‍🏫 Teacher only · Activity idea · Extension (T17)
Students take turns to close their books and remember as many of the items in Exercise 7 as possible. To get a point, they have to say both the adjective and the noun. Teacher’s Book: Check/clarify: roommate. If space allows, encourage students to walk around the classroom to find someone who could be their perfect roommate.
