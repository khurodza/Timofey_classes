Meet the sounds

c and n

Cc
/k/
c — as in cat

How to say it

The back of your tongue goes up, then a puff of air: k! Usually the letter c sounds just like k.

Listen and repeat

German trap: c = “ts”?German eyes read c before e / i as “ts” (Cäsar). In English it is /s/:
Rule: c + a, o, u → /k/ · c + e, i, y → /s/ · never “ts”!
Nn
/n/
n — as in net

How to say it

The tip of your tongue goes up behind your top teeth. The sound comes out through your nose. The same as German!

Listen and repeat
